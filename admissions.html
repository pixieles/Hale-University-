Requirements

Undergraduate

Secondary school diploma, minimum 3.2 GPA, personal essay, and two recommendations.

Graduate

Bachelor's degree, research proposal, CV, and three recommendations.

International

Certified translations and proof of English proficiency, plus a visa support letter from our office.
